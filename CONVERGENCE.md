# Continue until the evidence supports the decision

The frozen entry gate is 12 fresh good and 12 fresh bad openings. The five
independent unseen briefs are an operating pilot. Neither establishes
production accuracy. Debug on development cases as long as there is a
testable cause. If a qualification label informs a change, the revised
version needs a fresh qualification set.

The independent unit for generalization is the brief/topic. Group seeds and
edits of one clip as related measurements. Include the intended mixture of
exact, documentary, illustrative, numerical and qualitative cases. Compare
reviewers on identical MP4s and identical observation packets. Compare
writers on identical briefs with voice, reviewer, and settings frozen. If
both changed, adjudicate both MP4s under both reviewer versions. Report
improved, regressed, and unchanged pairs; do not use higher acceptance as
evidence of higher accuracy. Any relaxed rule needs a viewer-facing
justification and a predeclared tolerance for new bad approvals.

Report exact one-sided 95% bounds for each fixed-sample production metric:

| Target | Independent observations needed with zero failures |
|---|---:|
| At least 95% unattended publishable yield | 59 eligible unseen briefs |
| At least 99% correctly caught and diagnosed bad films | 299 independently bad films |
| At most 5% unnecessary withholds | 59 independently good films |

Do not repeatedly check a fixed-sample bound and stop when it crosses a
threshold. Freeze the sample size or choose a valid sequential procedure in
advance. Predeclare a joint rule if asserting all three claims jointly.
Retain every frozen run. Audit actual representative releases as well as
planted errors. Viewer preference and comprehension are unmeasured without
independent viewing; rendered controls and rehearsal viewers are not
substitutes. Opening and full-film metrics remain separate.

## Final report

Lead with publishable unchanged and locally accepted with zero intervention
(split first attempts and bounded repairs), bad films incorrectly accepted,
and good films unnecessarily withheld. Give numerators, denominators, and
confidence bounds. For zero submissions, the rate and bound are undefined.

Record each substantive change, including rejected and inconclusive tests:

| Change and version | Failure and verified cause | Change and viewer/operator consequence | Matched before → after | Unfamiliar result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Explain whether the film, the accuracy of a decision on the same film, or
the definition of a pass changed. Distinguish verified individual repairs,
matched development results, and independent qualification. End with
what is reliable, the most costly failure, and the dominant release
uncertainty. Provide the exact XPS folder plus a timed before/after pair.
