# Communication evaluation v1

GPT‑6.2 Sol owns adjudication. One caller submission, one immutable brief,
one model/skill per arm. Render and writer internal attempts remain visible.
Use each model's required chat template; fix the voice (Heart), native
preflight, service commits, signatures, sampling settings, and repair budget
within a round. Never expose an oracle to the writer.

1. Blind stage: present anonymous scripts first; on the video track, present
   actual timed pictures plus playable audio when available. Record what
   message came across, who it seemed for, and what conveyed it before
   revealing the target. State exactly what media was available. A transcript
   is not evidence about voice delivery or pacing perceived by a listener.
2. Reveal the brief and oracle. Record separate script and video verdicts for
   facts, objective, takeaway, tone, clarity, and feasibility. Inspect the
   decoded phone-size opening, each real cue immediately before/at, changes
   in captions, and closing hold. Validate the MP4 hash, duration and speech
   evidence. Wrong facts, missing decisive evidence, false associations,
   premature disclosure where material, or wrong cue timing fail the video.
3. Keep `verified_pass`, `incorrect_accepted`, writer/renderer refusal,
   `needs_input`, operational failure, and unjudged distinct. Report
   first-candidate vs automatic repair and under 30 s separately. All
   delivered MP4s must be strictly below 45 s. Only human viewers can
   substantiate audience comprehension or emotional response.

No validation tuning with the sealed briefs. Keep siblings together. The six
converted legacy briefs are B01/B03 comparison and B04/B05/B16/B18 education;
the other fourteen remain separate regressions. Report matched results when
changing the writer; lock the same story when comparing renderer changes.
